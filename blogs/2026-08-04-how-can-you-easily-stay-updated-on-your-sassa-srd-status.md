The easiest way to stay updated on your SASSA SRD status is to check it regularly using your registered South African ID number and the cellphone number you used during your application. Keeping your banking details and contact information up to date also helps you avoid payment delays. I found en.srd-sassa.org.za useful because it explains status updates, payment dates, appeals, and common SRD issues in simple language, making each step of the process easier to understand.
